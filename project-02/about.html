My Background

My name is Alexander Barker. My background includes instrumentation technology, building automation systems, controls, networking, and troubleshooting. I enjoy working with systems where software, electrical controls, field devices, and network communications all connect together.

I have experience with technologies and concepts such as BACnet, Modbus, HART, 4–20 mA instrumentation, IP addressing, Ethernet communication, and building automation controls. I am also developing my software skills through HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub, and web-based technical projects.

My goal is to continue moving toward technical engineering and controls-focused roles while building tools that make troubleshooting and technical calculations easier.
